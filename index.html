What Your Hormones Mean

📊 LH:FSH ratio > 2 is a common marker in PCOS, indicating the ovaries may be producing more androgens.

📈 Elevated AMH (>3.5 ng/mL) reflects a higher number of antral follicles, typical in PCOS.

⚠️ High fasting insulin suggests insulin resistance — a major driver of PCOS symptoms.

💡 Always consult your gynaecologist or endocrinologist before making any treatment decisions based on these values.
